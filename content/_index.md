# STUPHOS - STUdent of PHOtonicS
## at the University of Trento

📧 [students-of-photonics@unitn.it](mailto:students-of-photonics@unitn.it)

> **"Deep Dive into Light in the Alpine Atmosphere"**

Welcome to the official website of the Students of Photonics (STUPHOS) at the University of Trento!

We are a passionate community of Bachelor’s, Master’s, and PhD students from the University of Trento (UniTN) and Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK), united by our fascination with Optics and Photonics. Surrounded by the beautiful Alpine landscape, we aim to bridge the gap between academic research and real-world impact.

As a self-organized student group, we are dedicated to:
* **Exploring Innovation**: Discussing cutting-edge scientific research and the latest technological developments in the field.
* **Building Community**:   Organizing engaging events, workshops, and networking activities for students and professionals.
* **Inspiring the Public**: Sharing our passion with the broader, non-expert community.

>💡 Did you know? STUPHOS is an officially recognized Student Chapter of both SPIE. and OPTICA, and we are proudly supported by their international networks.
